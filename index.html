Privacy Policy for TNS Android Controller

Effective Date: October 30, 2025
Last Updated: October 30, 2025

Summary: TNS Android Controller operates entirely locally on your device and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Your privacy is protected by design.

Overview

TNS Android Controller ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Android device management application.

Information We Collect

Information We DO NOT Collect

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, emails, or user accounts
  • No Usage Analytics: We do not track your usage patterns or app behavior
  • No Device Information: We do not collect information about your computer or Android devices
  • No Network Data: We do not monitor or log your network activities

Local Data Only

  • ADB Commands: Commands are executed locally on your device and are not transmitted anywhere
  • Device Connections: All Android device connections are handled locally through Android Debug Bridge (ADB)
  • Temporary Files: Any temporary files created are stored locally and automatically cleaned up

How We Use Information

Since we do not collect any personal information, there is no data to use, share, or process. All operations are performed locally on your device.

Data Storage and Security

  • Local Processing: All operations are performed locally on your Windows device
  • No Cloud Storage: No data is stored in the cloud or on remote servers
  • No Data Transmission: No information is sent to external servers or third parties

Third-Party Services

  • Android Debug Bridge (ADB): The app uses Google's ADB tool for Android device communication. ADB operations are performed locally and follow Google's ADB protocols
  • No Analytics Services: We do not use any analytics, crash reporting, or tracking services
  • No Advertising: The app does not contain advertisements or ad tracking

Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is designed for technical users managing Android devices and does not target children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
